Methodology & source protection

Every calculated figure must be backed by provider-supplied UTC M1 OHLC data at the exact event and comparison timestamps. No interpolation, no estimated pips, and no prediction is used. Forex pips use 0.0001 (EURUSD/GBPUSD) and 0.01 (USDJPY); Gold and Bitcoin display their quote-currency price change instead. Records require administrator approval and confirmed display rights before public release. Market information is educational, not a trading recommendation.
